# sous — INT8 K/V through the attention tile (the `kv_int8` mirror)

*2026-10-12. The in-tree design for the L4 lever of the block-5 round profile (profile report of 2026-10-12, the maintainer's `sous-spikes/prof-b5/results.md`) and its stage-1 spike (`sous-spikes/l4-int8kv/results.md`, same day). Grounded on the maintainer's M5 Pro (applegpu_g17s, 20 GPU cores, macOS 27.0, mlx 0.32.3, mlx-vlm 0.7.2, the default Qwen3.8-27B-4bit with DFlash2). The maintainer ruled "take i8q to stage 2 and PR the relay first" on 2026-10-12; the split-QK relay is its own PR (#181, `feat/attention-tile-split-qk`), this design assumes it is in main, and every ratio below is against that relay tile. Before commit, two rounds of adversarial review (five reviewers) checked the drafts against the tree, mlx-vlm 0.7.2's source and the spike's data; their findings are folded in, and the departures from the spike's registered stage-2 shape are listed at the end.*

## What the measurements established

**The attention tile is bandwidth-bound at T = 1 and 2 and issue-bound from the second 16-row fragment group (T = 3) on.** With the relay, its time at 57K keys per 16-layer chain is 13.2–13.3 ms at T = 1 and 13.8 ms at T = 5, reading K/V at 270–283 GB/s; before the relay a window diagnostic (every address pinned to one 32-key block) still cost 0.93 of the streaming time at T = 5, and the relay changed T ≤ 5 by nothing.

**Halving the K/V bytes alone does not pay at T = 5.** The spike's first variant (int8 K/V, the tile's load pattern) was 1.8× at T = 1 and 0.83× at T = 3..5. Three further changes, each measured on the ktime chain with an A/A pair, carried it over the bar (ms per chain at 57K; `bf16` is the relay tile, `bf16s` in the spike, 13.79 ms at T = 5):

| variant | T = 5 | ratio | T = 1 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| relay tile (in-tree after #181) | 13.79 | 1.00 | 13.26 | 1.00 |
| int8 K/V, 16-byte V and scale loads (26 load instructions per lane-block, not 48) | 11.58 | 1.19 | 7.23 | 1.83 |
| + split-QK relay | 10.31 | 1.34 | 7.03 | 1.89 |
| + int8 Q, QK on the `int8_t × int8_t → int32_t` rows (`i8q`) | **8.17** | **1.69** | 7.33 | 1.81 |

`i8q` is 1.65× at 44K and 1.72× at 80K at T = 5; at T = 6 and 8 it is 1.16× the relay tile (the relay already removed most of the third fragment group's cost; the int8 kernel brings part of it back). It comes from one timing job (22 paired rounds per cell); the replicate with a new buffer seed and the variant order reversed covered the variants without int8 Q and reproduced their ratios within 0.015. On the real 35K-token tools fork `i8q` is 1.60× at T = 5. Converting int8 to bf16 in registers instead of feeding MPP's mixed rows is 0.80× at T = 5. Two things this design changes were not timed in the spike and are decided by the gate's kernel step, with fallbacks pre-registered there: the spike quantised Q on the host (about nine small mlx ops per layer), this design does it in the kernel; the spike read separate K and V buffers, this design reads one packed buffer.

**Quantisation error is small on real K/V.** After `k_norm` the keys have no outlier channels (the largest channel's amax is 2.5–4.9× the median channel's), so one symmetric scale per (token, KV head) costs 1.0 % relative RMS on K and V. On the fork's 16 full-attention layers, the fp32 attention output with int8 K/V against bf16 K/V is 0.73 % median / 1.76 % max with channel-matched Gaussian queries and 0.86 % / 1.59 % with peaked queries (`kvstats`); per layer it runs from 0.1–0.2 % (layers 3, 7, 63) to 1.0–1.8 % (layers 19–31). Group-64 scales cost 0.46–0.67 % median by the same measure; they do not fold into the tile's permutation and stay the fallback if the suite objects. The spike's kernel-level quality check ran on one layer (layer 3): 0.15 % with Gaussian queries and 0.71 % with peaked queries against the bf16 tile; int8 Q raised the Gaussian figure from 0.08 % to 0.15 % there. The gate's quality step runs the final kernel over all 16 layers.

**The kernel is exact in the tile's sense.** Every verify row equals its one-row call bit for bit (235/235 rows at N0, the next two step boundaries, 35,001 real tokens and 57,601, T ∈ {1, 3, 5, 8}), the NaN fence past n holds, and the output is within 2.3e-3 relative RMS of an fp32 reference on the same codes (K, V and Q).

**Per-round quantisation is dispatch-bound.** Quantising a round's five appended rows for 16 layers costs 0.46 ms in absolute terms through a fused kernel plus two slice updates per layer (48 dispatches; 1.65 ms through plain mlx ops), against the stage-1 bar of 0.30 ms that the maintainer ruled past, and against a 5.6 ms saving per block-5 round at 57K. The one-time build of the mirror for a 57K prefix is bandwidth-bound: 57,000 rows × 4 heads × (1,024 B read + 516 B written) × 16 layers = 5.6 GB, 20 ms at 280 GB/s as a single pass; it scales to 46 ms at 131K and 92 ms at 262K. The spike's int8 saving at small contexts (0.58 ms per 16-layer chain at 8K, T = 5, before the relay) is near the per-round quantisation cost, so kv8 may need its own floor above N0; the gate's kernel step decides it and this spec fixes the floor's shape in advance.

## Decisions

1. **An int8 mirror beside the bf16 cache, built and read only inside `decode()`.** The KVCache stays bf16 and is what every `prefill()` call, the forks, the fork store, the slots and every existing gate see. Inside `VLMEngine.decode()` the kv8 path serves one-row decode and verify attention at n ≥ N8 (below) from an int8 copy of the committed rows that lives on the cache object for that call only. That includes the one-row forward that ends the decode call's own prefill segment (`generate_step`'s final `_step` over the last prompt token, inside `stream_generate`: the seven-token generation prompt on the hybrid path, the whole prompt on the cold paths): its KV never outlives the turn, because the hybrid path restores the anchor snapshot after the decode and the cold paths discard the cache (qwen3_5 caches are never all trimmable, so the trimmable branch that would keep one is unreachable). Nothing kv8 serves reaches a stored fork or a slot.
2. **Per-token symmetric int8 with fp16 scales for K and V; int8 Q with an fp32 scale per row, quantised inside the kernel.** Scale = amax / 127, round to nearest even, codes in [−127, 127]; a scale that rounds to 0 divides by 1 instead, so an all-zero or underflowing row gives codes 0 (K, V and Q alike), and a scale above fp16's maximum saturates there. The K scale multiplies the finished score, the V scale folds into the probability, the Q scale multiplies the score: no dequantisation in the loop.
3. **The verify rows read the same codes as decode rows.** A (row, key) score depends on the row's codes and scale, the key's codes and scale, and nothing else; the mirror is a pure per-row function of the bf16 rows, so bulk and incremental quantisation agree bit for bit, and a verify row equals the one-row call at its own length. Greedy output with the drafter equals greedy output without it, as for the tile.
4. **The mirror follows the cache's offset through `trim`, not through the calls kv8 happens to serve.** Inside a decode the only thing that lowers a KVCache offset is `KVCache.trim`, which DFlash's commit and abort reach as an instance call; the mirror wraps it on the instance and shrinks with it. Served calls also clamp to the committed prefix and a mirror from another decode is rebuilt from row 0, but those are defences; the trim wrap is the mechanism, and the source pins plus a contract test through the real round loop guard it.
5. **One flag, `[model].kv_int8`, default `true`, refused where the tile is not active.** The kernel rides on the tile (its platform gate, S, the chunk schedule, `verify_plan`, `MAX_RUN`, the reduce kernel and, with a drafter, exact verify attention), so it inherits every tile refusal. A typo warns and means `false`.
6. **Memory: deterministic admission by context, sized so the turn's bf16 KV and its mirror fit the reserve together; the reserve, the slot budget and the pressure valve do not change.** The mirror costs 0.504 × the bf16 KV of the rows it covers (516 B per token per head against 1,024). Scaling the window-sized reserve by that would cut the automatic slot budget by 4–8 GiB (16–45 % on the M5 Pro at the 131072 and 262144 windows) and buy cache misses that cost more than the lever saves; admitting against live memory would make the same prompt's output depend on slot occupancy. So a decode admits kv8 if and only if `cap × 1.504 ≤ window`, where `cap` is the mirror capacity the decode would need (below) and `window` is `[model].max_context_tokens`: the mirror then sits inside the reserve beside the turn's own KV, and the decision is a function of the request alone. Past that length (about 87K at a 131072 window, 174K at 262144) the decode runs on the bf16 tile and the turn line says so.
7. **The fork epoch changes once with this PR; later kernel-only edits do not cold-start the store.** `vlm.py` and `tileattn.py` are hashed into the epoch and both gain seams here, so deploying this cold-starts the fork store once (a cold first turn was 175 s against 15.7 s from disk). Nothing kv8 serves is persisted (decision 1), so the key gains no field, `kv8.py` stays out of `_EPOCH_FILES`, and the two new kernel sources are excluded from the epoch by name, as `verifyattn.py` is: the glob would otherwise cold-start the store on every later edit to kernels that never touch stored KV. Deploying #181 and this together pays one cold store instead of two.
8. **`sous tune` keys its suite rows by the flag** (`Arm.kv_int8`, `SuiteRun.kv_int8`), as it does for the tile and the projection kernel, because the flag changes numerics; the gate's suite step builds its on/off arm pair out of tree, as #123's harness did.

## Design

### Module layout

- `src/sous/engine/kv8.py` — the serving window, the mirror and its trim wrap, the quantiser dispatch, the kernel dispatch, `availability()`, `enable()`, `clear()`, the probe, `calls`, the source pins. It imports `tileattn` at module level (for `N0`, `GRAN`, `chunk_for`, `verify_plan`, `boundaries`, the reduce kernel and the constants); `tileattn` imports `kv8` function-locally inside the two seams, so there is no cycle. mlx imports stay function-local throughout.
- `src/sous/engine/kernels/attention_tile_kv8.metal` — the split body over int8 K/V/Q (the relay kernel with int8 operands), kernel name `sous_kv8_tile_split`; the tile's reduce kernel is reused unchanged.
- `src/sous/engine/kernels/kv8_quant.metal` — the fused row quantiser, kernel name `sous_kv8_quant` (MPP-free: `common.h` only, so it compiles on any Metal GPU and CI tests it).
- Seams in existing files: `tileattn._serve_decode` and `tileattn.serve_verify` (which gains the `cache` argument `verifyattn._wrap` already holds at its only call site); `VLMEngine` enables it after the projection kernel, opens the serving window in `decode()`, drops the mirrors and clears the window in `decode()`'s `finally`, clears it in `unload()`; `LMEngine` carries an `off` status and the default `Speculation`; `ManagedEngine` passes the status through by `getattr`; `base._default_factory` and `base.default_engine_factory` pass the config; `config.py` parses the key; the load line, the status document and the turn line carry its state; `promptcache.Speculation`, `TURN_GAUGES`, `PromptCacheStats`, `_note_speculation`, `api/turn.py` and `api/routes.py` carry the per-turn gauges; `sous tune` keys rows by it; `forkstore.engine_epoch` excludes the two kernel files by name.

### Module state and the serving window

- `_active: int` — 1 only after `enable()` passed every gate and the probe; the first statement of every `enable()` clears it; `clear()` clears it; the probe raises it, with the window, only while it runs.
- `_window: Window | None` — the open decode, or None. `Window(serial: int, cap: int, admitted: bool, rows: int, served: int)`: `serial` increases with every window; `cap` is the mirror capacity this decode needs; `admitted` is decision 6's verdict; `rows` counts rows quantised in this decode; `served` counts kv8-served calls. `VLMEngine.decode()` opens it with `kv8.open_window(cache, max_tokens, block)` immediately before `stream_generate` (inside the draft-context `seed` block, so the whole generation, its own prefill segment included, is covered) only when `_active`, and in `finally` calls `kv8.close_window(cache)`, which records the window's gauges into the engine's `Speculation`, drops every full-attention layer's mirror and its trim wrap, clears `_window` and calls `mx.clear_cache()` (the session thread; the mirror's buffers would otherwise sit in mlx's cache through the publish that follows the decode). An exception out of `stream_generate` reaches the same `finally`.
- `serving_now()` is `_active and _window is not None and _window.admitted`. A module global suffices because `ManagedEngine._gen_lock` serialises every forward and every `decode()` call site (the two hybrid-path calls and the two cold-path calls in `promptcache`) runs on the caller's session thread. Every `prefill()` call, the load-time probes and anything outside `decode()` run with no window, so they never build or read a mirror.
- `cap` for a decode is `round_up(n0 + max_tokens + block + 1, 256)` with `n0` the cache offset when the window opens, `max_tokens` the decode's argument and `block` the pinned draft block (0 without a drafter); the mirror is built at that capacity and never grows inside a decode (`calls["mirror_grown"]` counts the growth path that handles a cap exceeded anyway, by doubling; a test asserts a decode performs none). Admission is `cap × 1.504 ≤ window`.

### The floor N8

kv8 serves from `N8` keys, a tile step boundary at or above N0: `N8 = N0` unless the gate's kernel step shows the int8 saving net of per-round quantisation below zero at some served context, in which case `N8` is the first element of `tileattn.boundaries(...)` at or past the first context where it is above zero. Because every step boundary is already a `verify_plan` cut, no verify run straddles N8, so a call mixes the kv8 kernel and the bf16 tile only across runs, each of which equals its one-row calls. `N8` is a module constant read by both seams, the probe's parity marks include N8 and the next boundary, and the docs say "from N8 keys". Below N8 (and below N0) the paths are the tile's and the stock ones, unchanged.

### The mirror

A `Mirror` object attached to a full-attention layer's `KVCache` as `_sous_kv8` (a plain attribute set with `object.__setattr__`, like the left-padding attributes mlx-vlm itself sets; `KVCache` has no slots or `__setattr__`, and `fork_copy` copies `state` and `meta_state` into a fresh cache, so a mirror never travels with a fork):

- `codes`: int8 `[1, 4, cap, 512]`, each token's 256 K codes then its 256 V codes (the kernel reads K and V as strided views of this one buffer: token stride 512 B, V at +256; every 16-byte load stays aligned because 512 and 256 are multiples of 16);
- `sc`: fp16 `[1, 4, cap, 2]` = {sK, sV} per (token, head), contiguous in its last two axes;
- `cap`: the window's capacity, a multiple of 256. It is deliberately not `keys.shape[2]`: `KVCache.update_and_fetch` truncates to the offset before it grows, so a cache's capacity is routinely not a multiple of 4 after the first irregular prefill segment, and the kernel's 16-byte scale loads need the per-head plane stride (`cap × 4 B`) to be a multiple of 16;
- `n`: the number of valid rows, from 0;
- `serial`: the window serial it was built under;
- `trim`: the wrapped instance method (an instance attribute shadowing the class method; `cache_state._trim_append_caches` resolves `getattr(cache, "trim")` and `SpeculativeCacheTransaction.abort` calls `cache.trim`, so both commit and abort reach it): after the original runs, `n = min(n, cache.offset)` and `calls["mirror_trims"] += 1`.

**Validity.** `n` moves down by the trim wrap (the mechanism: inside a decode nothing else lowers a KVCache offset — `prefix_cache_reserve` pads by concatenation and keeps rows and offset, and the `state` setter is reached only by `restore` and `fork_copy`, which run outside `decode()`), by the clamp every kv8-served call runs first (`n = min(n, n_keys − T)`: rows below `n_keys − T` are the committed prefix), and by the serial (a mirror from another window is rebuilt from row 0). It moves up when a served call at `n_keys ≥ N8` quantises rows `[n, n_keys)` and sets `n = n_keys`. The first served call of a window quantises the whole prefix with one quantiser dispatch per layer that writes the cap-sized `codes` and `sc` directly (no zero-fill, no slice update; rows at or past `n` are never read because every kernel read is clamped to `n_keys − 1`); later served calls quantise the new rows into a row-sized output and slice-assign it (two updates per layer; the mirror's buffers are referenced from the `Mirror` only, so mlx donates them and the update is in place — a test bounds the update's time).

**Lifetime.** Built on the first served call inside a window at `n_keys ≥ N8`, dropped by `close_window`; the probe's `finally` drops what it built.

### The quantiser (`kv8_quant.metal`, `sous_kv8_quant`)

Inputs `k`, `v` (the cache's bf16 views `keys[:, :, a:b, :]`, read through their strides with `ensure_row_contiguous=False`) and `params` int32 `{rows, out_rows}`; outputs `codes` int8 `[1, 4, out_rows, 512]` and `sc` fp16 `[1, 4, out_rows, 2]`; template none; grid `(256, rows, 4)`, threadgroup `(256, 1, 1)`. Thread d of (head h, row r) reads `k[h*k_strides[1] + r*k_strides[2] + d]` and the same for `v`, takes the row's amax by `simd_max` and an 8-value threadgroup reduce, sets `s = half(amax / 127)` saturating at fp16's maximum, uses `f = (s > 0) ? float(s) : 1`, writes `codes[(h*out_rows + r)*512 + d] = char(clamp(rint(x / f), −127, 127))` for K and at `+256` for V, and from thread 0 `sc[(h*out_rows + r)*2] = {sK, sV}`. The first build calls it with `rows = n_keys`, `out_rows = cap` (rows past `rows` are left unwritten and never read); an update calls it with `rows = out_rows = n_keys − n` and slice-assigns. Compiled with `common.h` only and `math_mode: safe`. The tests' reference quantiser is the same formula in mlx ops; `mx.round` is round-half-to-even in the installed mlx (checked on the CPU and GPU streams), the same rule as Metal's `rint`, and a CI test pins that vector so an mlx change fails loudly; tie rows (a power-of-two scale, so `x / s` lands on .5 exactly) are part of the tests because what could still differ at a tie is the division, not the rounding.

### The kernel (`attention_tile_kv8.metal`, `sous_kv8_tile_split`)

The relay kernel with these changes, everything else as the tile (grid, threadgroup, the 32·S chunk schedule, the online softmax, the fixed-order relay with its two barriers per block, resident accumulators, the clamp past n, the zero-probability fence, the partial/stats layout):

- **Inputs** `q` bf16 `[1, 24, T, 256]`, `codes` int8 and `sc` fp16 as above (read through strides, `ensure_row_contiguous=False`), `params`.
- **Q quantised in the kernel, staged as codes.** In a prologue before the accumulators are declared, each lane loads its two rows' 8 k-steps of Q (16 `bfloat4`), takes its amax per row, reduces over the four lanes sharing a row (`simd_shuffle_xor` 1 and 8), exchanges the two halves' row maxima through threadgroup memory (one barrier), sets `sq = amax / 127` in fp32 per row with the zero guard of decision 2, and writes its values as packed int8 codes (`clamp(rint(q / f), −127, 127)`, one `uint` per lane per row per k-step) into the staging array, which is therefore 4 KB per row group as the spike's; each lane reads back only what it wrote, so no second barrier is needed. One barrier per call beyond the relay's two per block.
- **QK** on `get_left_input_cooperative_tensor<int8_t, int8_t, int32_t>` / right the same, `multiply_accumulate` into an int32 destination zeroed per block; the relay sums int32 partials (exact: |Si| ≤ 256 × 127² < 2²⁴); then `S = float(Si) × (sq[row] × (SCALE_LOG2 × sK[key]))`, the spike's association, which the stand-in reproduces.
- **Loads per lane per 32-key block:** K as 16-byte runs (two per key row per half), V as 16-byte runs covering two output tiles, scales as one 16-byte load per 16-key half when the whole block lies below n and clamped `half2` loads otherwise. Every K, V and scale read is clamped to `n − 1`: that clamp, not the mask, keeps a NaN or garbage row past n out (0 × NaN is NaN; the mask only zeroes probabilities of keys whose scale the clamp kept finite).
- **PV** on `<float, int8_t, float>` rows: `ct_p = p × sV[key]`, the row sum on the unscaled p.
- **MSL** as the tile: `remove_addrspace_t` on every cooperative-tensor `decltype`, `math_mode: safe`, `common.h + nax.h` as the header. `availability()` is the shared platform rule and then a compile-and-run of every T = 1..8 and of the quantiser, success remembered only, so kv8's `nax` tests skip on kv8's own availability.
- **Pre-registered fallbacks**, decided by gate step 2 in this order if the in-kernel Q prologue or the packed layout costs more than the spike's 1.69× allows: (a) a two-pass Q prologue (amax pass, then reload and quantise four values at a time, no 16-register hold); (b) host-quantised Q as the spike timed it (about nine mlx ops per layer); (c) separate K and V planes.

### Dispatch (`kv8.py` and the seams)

Served predicate: `serving_now() and n_keys ≥ N8`.

- `tileattn._serve_decode`, after its existing scope checks: `if keys.shape[2] < N0: decode_stock; return None`; then `if kv8.serving_now() and keys.shape[2] >= kv8.N8: calls["decode_tile"] untouched; return kv8.decode(queries, cache, splits)`; else the tile as today. `kv8.decode(queries, cache, splits)` clamps and updates the mirror from `cache.keys`/`cache.values`/`cache.offset` (`n_keys = cache.offset`, T = 1), counts `decode_kv8`, and runs the kv8 kernel through the tile's chunk schedule.
- `tileattn.serve_verify(queries, keys, values, splits, cache)`: `verify_plan` and the tile's call counting as today (`verify_calls`, `verify_t*`, `verify_straddles`); then for each run: a run with chunk 0 takes the stock path (`verify_rows_stock`); a run at or above N8 while serving goes to `kv8.verify_run(queries, cache, j, k, chunk)` (`verify_kv8` once per call that sent any run there, `verify_rows_kv8`, `verify_launches_kv8`); any other run takes the tile (`verify_rows_tile`, `verify_launches`). `kv8.verify_run` clamps and updates the mirror once per call (T = the call's rows). A verify call whose runs all fall below N8 is not a kv8-served call.
- Counters `kv8.calls`: `decode_kv8`, `verify_kv8`, `verify_rows_kv8`, `verify_launches_kv8`, `mirror_builds`, `mirror_rows`, `mirror_trims`, `mirror_grown`, `windows_admitted`, `windows_declined`. Per decode, `close_window` records `kv8 = "served" | "declined" | "off"` and `kv8_rows` into `Speculation` (two new fields, defaulting to `"off"` and 0 in `LMEngine.speculation()`), `_note_speculation` copies them into the gauges `kv8` and `kv8_rows`, and the turn line prints `kv8=served kv8_rows=57005`. The build's time is measured in the gate's kernel step, not per turn.
- `enable(model, *, enabled, tile_status, verify_status)`: first statement clears `_active` and `_window`; `off` → `{"state": "off", "reason": None, "probe_seconds": None}`; `unavailable` with one INFO line (`expected=True`) when the tile is not `active` (its reason or `attention tile off`); then the source pins, `availability()`, and the probe on the loading thread; a compile or probe failure, pin drift and any exception (logged with its traceback) are `warnings.warn` plus `unavailable`. The probe raises `_active` and opens a window only while it runs, and in `finally` closes the window, drops the mirrors it built, clears `_active`, restores `kv8.calls`, `tileattn.calls` and `projkernel.calls`, and calls `mx.clear_cache()`, before the engine measures the budget. The number of full-attention layers is read from the model at enable (a plain int) for the admission arithmetic.
- The probe, numbered (inputs are GPU-built and evaluated before any launch; the fp32 reference is fp32 SDPA on the GPU over the dequantised K, V and Q codes): (1) quantiser against the reference on random rows, tie rows and an all-zero row, bitwise; bulk equals incremental (one build against rows added in two steps); (2) kernel exactness: relative RMS ≤ 1e-2 against the reference at N8 and the far mark, T = 1, 5, 8, including ×8 queries with a dominant key at n − 1 and a decoy at n; (3) parity: every verify row `array_equal` its one-row call at N8, the next two step boundaries and the far mark, T = 1..8 at the tile's three placements; (4) fence: NaN scales and −128 codes past n, and buffers ending exactly at n, bitwise equal to the clean run and finite; (5) the mirror rule on a real `KVCache`: build, `trim` through the wrap, append without a served call, append with one; the shrinking-block sequence across N8 (16 rows served, trim below N8, 8 rows below N8 unserved, then a served call) ends with the mirror equal to a bulk re-quantisation; a stale serial rebuilds; a cache capacity not a multiple of 4 is handled; (6) quantisation error against stock bf16 SDPA on Gaussian K/V ≤ 3e-2 (the format's own error there is about 0.7 %); (7) the model's own attention module through the decode hook with a window open, `calls` showing `decode_kv8`, within 3e-2 of the same module on the bf16 path; and, only when `verify_status` is active, the same module through the verifier's wrapper (`_verifier_class()()._attention(module, x, "causal", cache, None, None)` on a restored-style `KVCache`, as projkernel's probe does) with `verify_kv8` moving. Without a drafter kv8 serves decode only, and the load line says so through the tile's own status.
- Source pins `VALIDATED_KV8_SOURCES` (sha256 of `inspect.getsource`, following `__wrapped__`, module and qualname as `verifyattn._source_digest` takes them): `mlx_vlm.models.cache`: `KVCache.trim`, `KVCache.prefix_cache_reserve`; `mlx_vlm.speculative.cache_state`: `start_speculative_cache`, `SpeculativeCacheTransaction.commit`, `SpeculativeCacheTransaction.abort`, `_trim_append_caches`, `_cache_position_reader`, `commit_speculative_round`, `abort_speculative_round`, `rollback_speculative_cache`; `mlx_vlm.speculative.dflash`: `_dflash_rounds`, `_dflash_verify`; `mlx_vlm.models.qwen3_5.language`: `LanguageModel.rollback_speculative_cache`; `mlx_vlm.models.qwen3_5.speculative_verifier`: `Qwen3_5BatchInvariantForward.verify`. The mirror's validity assumes verify rows are appended through `update_and_fetch` and rolled back through the instance `trim`, never replayed; mlx-vlm's rotating-cache path already replays accepted rows, and a KVCache moved onto such a path would go stale with every gate green. Checked in `enable()` (a warn refusal) and by a CI hash test like verifyattn's.
- `clear()` from `VLMEngine.unload()`: `_active` and `_window`, nothing else (mirrors live on caches that are dropped with the engine).

### Engine, config, status, tune

- `config.py`: `kv_int8: bool = True` on `SousConfig`, parsed by `_kv_int8` with the warn-and-False rule; `_KNOWN["model"]` gains the key.

## Merge gate (pre-registered 2026-10-12, before any in-model number)

Preconditions: main at or after the relay merge (#181); the branch tree in its own worktree and venv on the M5 Pro; every job under `gpu_run_m5.py` with the production daemon idle. Verdict: **ship** only if steps 1–6 all pass; a failure in a step with a close or keep-off rule closes as that step says; anything else goes to the maintainer.

1. **Tests:** `uv run pytest -m "not model"` green on the M2 and the M5 (nax tests included), `uv run pytest -m model tests/test_kv8_model.py tests/test_tileattn_model.py` green on the M5; ty, ruff, lock clean.
2. **Kernel timings** (ktime chain, A/A pair within ±1 %, the final kernel with in-kernel Q over the packed layout against the relay tile): T = 1..8 at 57K; T = 1, 3, 5, 6, 8 at 44K, 80K and 131K; T = 1 and 5 at N0, 4K, 8K and 16K: ≥ 1.35× at (5, 57K), (1, 57K) and (5, 80K); r ≥ 0.97 at every T at 44K and above; and a dependent-chain cell per context where each layer runs the quantiser for 5 rows, its two slice updates and the kernel (the per-round cost the model pays) plus a single-dispatch build cell per context (the first build's cost, `mirror_rows` = the context): the kernel saving minus the per-round cost must be ≥ 0 at every context kv8 serves, else N8 moves to the first step boundary at or past the first context where it is, and the parity probe and tests take the new marks. If (5, 57K) falls short of 1.35× the fallbacks run in the pre-registered order and the cell is re-timed.
3. **Speed, primary:** #131's 64 real subagent turns (context histogram reported, results stratified by 44K band) replayed through a real `sous serve` with a per-round shim (an instance wrap of the drafter's draft call stacked on draftctx's own, in the harness) that leaves the window as the engine opened it for the decode's own prefill segment (so the first build lands in TTFT, which step 4 reports) and then, per round, sets `_window.admitted` to a drawn value without touching the serial; off rounds leave the trim wrap active, so the next on round re-quantises the backlog and off rounds pay nothing; block 5, sampled at 0.7, two seeds; **ship** if the stratified dt-attributed tokens/s ratio on/off ≥ 1.02 with the 95 % turn-clustered bootstrap lower bound > 1.00 (#157's rule); **close** if the lower bound ≤ 1.00 and the point < 1.02; else to the maintainer. With `kv_int8 = false` the branch equals main (at or after #181) bitwise on the same replays.

## Departures from the spike's registered stage-2 shape (recorded here, with reasons)

- The mirror is built lazily inside `decode()` and follows trims, not written on every append: prefill never pays, forks never carry it, and the per-round gate shim becomes possible (the spike said the format could not be drawn per round).
- No fork-key field and no `_EPOCH_FILES` entry for `kv8.py`: nothing served is persisted; the epoch still changes once through the seams in `vlm.py` and `tileattn.py`.
- No reserve scaling: deterministic admission inside the reserve instead, for the slot-budget and reproducibility reasons in decision 6.
- Per-round arms are the primary speed gate and per-daemon ABBA the secondary, where the spike listed them the other way.
- Stage 1's rule (d) was missed by the mirror path (0.46 ms against 0.30) and the maintainer ruled past it; the gate's step 2 carries the cost as dependent-chain cells and may raise the floor N8 instead.

## Out of scope

- An int8-only cache (the memory win: smaller slots, forks and reserve). It needs a cache class forkio, fork_copy, every `type(cache) is KVCache` gate and the fork layout version admit; the mirror keeps those untouched and can become that later.
- Group-64 K scales (the quality fallback): needs the group-aligned head-dim permutation; built only if step 6 fails at per-token.
- Prefill attention over int8 history.
- Changing the default block: the relay PR's #157 retest decides that.
