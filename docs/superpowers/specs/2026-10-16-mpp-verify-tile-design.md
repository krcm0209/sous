# sous — MPP verify tile over slab weights (stage 1: decode)

*2026-10-16. The in-tree design for serving the DFlash round's verify projections from the M5 Pro's tensor units, from spike #126 (`sous-spikes/p126`, 2026-10-06, Splash 1.3.0's affine tile ported and tuned) and the two measurements of 2026-10-16 that reopened it (`sous-spikes/walk-gate`, `sous-spikes/slab`). The maintainer's rulings of 2026-10-16: greedy parity between the drafter on and off is relaxed to block invariance on the drafter's path; the decode tile ships before prefill moves onto slabs. Stage 2 (prefill on slabs for every linear, no second copy anywhere) is a later spec. Revised the same evening after a four-lens review (numerics, integration, gate, completeness); the changes are listed at the end.*

## What the measurements established

**The remaining Splash gap is step time, and step time is projections.** Same machine, same weights and drafter, 68 real subagent turns at verify block 8 (2026-10-14): Splash/sous decode = 1.331 [1.236, 1.444] = tokens/step 1.054 × step time 1.263 (sous 130 ms per step, Splash 103). The host side is closed: prof-b5 (2026-10-12) read a block-5 round as 96–98 % GPU-busy with host idle 2.7 % and the walk's GPU work about 1 ms, and the walk gate (2026-10-16) found the batched positioned walk worth +0.39 ms per round (time ratio 0.9989 [0.9932, 1.0038]). On the GPU a round at 58K is projections 70.7 ms (MLP 45.8, GDN 17.1, attention 4.9, head 2.8) = 72 %, the attention tile 13.6, the drafter 9.9, the GDN chain 3.0, the walk 1.0. At block 8 the in-tree projection kernel's forward is 72.7 ms (RULE sum over the seven dispatch keys, T = 8; the MMA computes an 8-row tile at every T, so T = 5 and T = 8 cost the same), and a block-8 round on the 44–88K replay set is 113.6 ms (the drafter-route gate, 2026-10-16).

**The MPP tile on Splash's slab layout is 0.835× the in-tree kernel, and neither half works alone.** #126's arm A — Splash 1.3.0's `q4_mpp_tiles.h` in `mx.fast.metal_kernel`, exact 4-bit codes in the tensor op, sous's two-fma epilogue per quant group, plans tuned over 1,440 candidates — ran the T = 5 forward in 61.0–64.1 ms against the kernel's 74.1–75.6 (0.848 / 0.833 / 0.824, mean 0.835); per key q+k+v 0.818, o/out 0.838, qkv+z 0.823, b+a 0.844, gate+up 0.855, down 0.800, lm_head 0.889, and the four-linear GDN stack 1.060, so the port serves b+a apart from qkv+z. Arm B — the same tensor-op kernel over mlx's own `[N, K/8]` words with only the scales re-tiled — was 1.022×. The slab spike (2026-10-16) closed the other direction: the in-tree MMA kernel re-addressed for the slab layout, bitwise identical on every output and on every cancelling layout, ran at 1.0065× (72.7 → 73.1 ms; 1.0066 / 1.0053 / 1.0076; gate+up 1.009–1.013, down 0.998), and a 256-column variant at 1.37×. The MMA kernel is compute-bound on the FP32 pipe whatever the bytes' order; the tensor op needs the contiguous 8 KB (tile, group) units to stream. The lever is both together. gate+up and down carry 63 % of the saving (7.8 of 12.3 ms per forward); the attention and GDN keys carry 4.2 ms; lm_head 0.3.

**Arm A is exact in the senses that matter and inexact in the one that no longer binds.** Every output row at T = 1..8 was bitwise equal to the same row alone, at every tuned plan (split plans included), on every dispatch key of the 27B's real weights (3,920 of 3,920 rows). Against a float64 reference every one of the 1,378 real linears sat at the bf16 floor: rel RMS 1.34e-3..2.12e-3, arm/qmv 1.0000, pooled excess over the floor 0.00017× the floor, zero element-bound violations. On the `pair` cancelling layout its excess over the floor equals the in-tree kernel's (0.96–1.04×); on `quarter` and `middle` it is 4–12× the kernel's and up to 1.39× qmv's (a single running fp32 sum over all G groups against the kernel's four K-quarter partials), still under 1e-2 relative (9.4e-3 at K = 17408). What it cannot do is reproduce the one-row kernel's bits: the tensor op adds adjacent product pairs first and no sequential IEEE fp32 order exists for its group dot (2,048 witness cells), and a padded one-row call costs 1.279× qmv. The verify's rows cannot equal one-row decode's bit for bit — the contract #126 closed on and the maintainer has now relaxed.

**Memory binds harder than the ruling message said.** The M5 Pro's Metal working set is 51.84 GiB, not 64; the resident 27B is 14.96 GiB before the drafter; the KV reserve at `max_context_tokens = 262144` is 16 GiB and the budget slack 2 GiB, so today's automatic slot budget is about 17 GiB (18.88 before the drafter). A second copy of every target linear (25.7 G weights at 9/16 byte, N padded to 256: 14.5 GB, 13.5 GiB) would leave about 4 GiB of prompt cache: one fork, no turn slot. So stage 1 cannot duplicate the model. It can duplicate the attention and GDN linears alone (7.3 G weights: 4.13 GB, 3.85 GiB — about 13 GiB of budget left at 262144, 21 at 131072), and the MLP linears, which carry 63 % of the saving, must hold one copy in the slab order and be served from it by every path that reads them. `docs/configuration.md` already puts the budget a fork needs beside a retaining conversation at about 14 GiB, and says a smaller one makes every cold turn pay a fork it never reuses; a 60–90K slot is 3.8–5.6 GiB and each fork 3.4–3.6.

**Expected effect.** 72.7 → 61–64 ms per forward is −9 to −12 ms of a 113.6 ms block-8 round: MLP alone about −7.8 ms (round 0.93×, step time against Splash from 1.26 to about 1.18), with attention and GDN about −12 ms (0.89×, about 1.13). Splash's own refinements of its staged tile (the spread K walk, 4–8 %; one-pass gate/up, 1.5–3.7 %, both new in 1.3.1 and measured only on its fp16-staged GGUF tile at 8-row lanes) and the drafter on the tile are follow-ons that address the rest; depth is closed (tokens/step 1.054).

## Decisions

1. **Inside a decode, every target projection call of the exact verifier, and every plain call of 2–8 rows, runs on an MPP tile over slab-ordered weights.** Hook 1 (the verifier's `_linear`/`_linears`) is served on the tile run by run after projkernel's existing cut into runs of at most 8 rows, a 1-row remainder included, so blocks above 8 (block 0 under the drafter's own policy, block 12 as the model test sets it) stay on one path; hook 2 (`QuantizedLinear.__call__`) is served on the tile at 2–8 rows — the decode call's own generation-prompt chunk is the one such call on a servable module, and the reason `plain_tile` exists. The tile serves only while a decode window is open, opened and closed where kv8's is. One-row calls (drafter-off decode, the one-row forward that ends every prefill segment) stay on the one-row kernel; prefill's calls of 128 rows and more stay on the int8 GEMM (MLP) and stock `quantized_matmul` (the rest); 2–8-row calls outside the window stay on the MMA kernel. **Every path keeps today's bits except one:** MLP calls of 9–127 rows, which stock `quantized_matmul` serves today and which a slab-only module cannot hand to it, move to the int8 GEMM (decision 3). So a stored fork, a turn slot and a drafter-off decode compute what they compute today, except the KV of a prefill chunk of 9–127 rows on a converted load, which is keyed (decision 7).
2. **The contract becomes block invariance on the drafter's path (the maintainer, 2026-10-16).** Exact speculative decoding needs the target's answer for a position not to depend on how many rows share the call or on the block size. The tile's rows are independent by construction (the kernel always computes a 16-row fragment from 8 padded rows; T is neither a template constant nor an argument) and the load probe proves it on real weights; the attention tile and kv8 already give a verify row the bits of its one-row call; the GDN verifier is batch-invariant. The oracle is therefore: **for a given load and scope, the same greedy prompt decoded with the drafter at block 8, at block 2 and at block 0 produces identical token streams**, checked in the gate on the 68 replay turns and, with block 12 besides, in a model test, plus the bf16 floor against a float64 reference on every linear and the suite. Drafter on against drafter off remains a bar, measured rather than assumed: both runs trace the top-2 token ids and raw bf16 logits at every committed token, the measured perturbation D is read over every position before a pair's first divergence, and a divergence passes only within D (gate step 6). Where the tile is refused or off, drafter on still equals drafter off bit for bit, as today, and the three existing model tests of that contract stay as they are.
3. **MLP linears (gate, up, down) are slab-only; attention and GDN linears get a slab copy, stacked as the verifier calls them, when the budget allows; `lm_head` stays as it is.** For gate, up and down the module's `weight`, `scales` and `biases` are *replaced* at load by the slab planes, and every path that reads them is re-addressed with its arithmetic unchanged: the int8 GEMM (the GEMM reads one uint2 per column and group from the same 32-byte unit and four adjacent scales that stay contiguous inside a 256-column tile; its activation K-order contract, `reorder_k` against the nibble decode, touches no weight address), the MMA kernel (2–8 rows outside the window, the slab spike's bodies, bitwise equal), the one-row kernel (a slab body with the one-row kernel's exact arithmetic, bitwise equal; 1.01–1.02× on gate+up and down), and the tile (inside the window). Calls of 9–127 rows on an MLP linear — a prefill segment whose last chunk leaves that many rows, which on the hybrid path includes every warm suffix of 10–128 tokens, the previous tool call plus its result — go to the int8 GEMM in one launch, with int8's row floor lowered for slab-only modules only (verify and decode never reach it); int8 is already required active, already in the fork key, and already what every longer chunk of those linears runs through. For q, k, v, o, in_proj_qkv, in_proj_z, in_proj_b, in_proj_a and out_proj a slab copy hangs off the module beside mlx's arrays when `enable()`'s memory guard allows it (below), stored as the stacks the spike tuned and the verifier calls: q+k+v (14336 columns) per attention layer, qkv+z (16384) and b+a (96 columns in one 256-column tile) per GDN layer, o and out_proj alone; their prefill paths are stock and keep mlx's layout. `lm_head` (0.3 ms of the 12.3, 0.7 GB, and read by mlx-vlm's `quantized_argmax` in mlx's layout) keeps mlx's layout and the MMA kernel at every row count, so the sampled drafter's seven-row call of it never reaches the tile.
4. **One key, `[model].projection_tile`: `true` (default, scope by the guard), `false`, `"mlp"` (never copy) or `"all"` (copy regardless, for a user who has read the budget).** `enable()` refuses (INFO, `unavailable`) wherever the projection kernel or the attention tile is not active, no DFlash drafter loaded (nothing the tile serves exists), int8 prefill is not active (a slab-only MLP linear has no 9-row-and-up path without it), or the GPU's core count is outside the plan table's measured set. Under `true` it serves the MLP linears always and the attention and GDN linears only when the slot budget left after their copy would be at least `TILE_COPY_MIN_BUDGET_GIB` = 14 — the documented figure for a fork kept beside a retaining conversation; a copy that evicts every fork costs more prefill than it saves decode. By that rule a 64 GB M5 Pro loads `active:mlp` at 262144 and `active:all` at 131072; the load line and the status document carry the scope, the reason the copy was skipped, and the copy's bytes, and gate step 4b measures what the copy costs the cache at 262144 so the constant can be revisited with data.
5. **The kernel is #126's arm A, in-tree, at its tuned plans; nothing is tuned at load, and nothing runs untuned.** Plans are a table keyed by dispatch key (K and the stacked N): q+k+v `TN128/S8/G80/pipe`; o/out `TN256/S8/G20/pipe ×2`; qkv+z `TN128/S8/G80/pipe`; b+a `TN128/S8/G2/pipe ×8`; gate+up dual `TN128/S4/G100/pipe`; down `TN256/S8/G20/pipe ×2`; the alone forms the window reaches through hook 2 — q `TN128/S8/G80/pipe`, k and v `TN128/S8/G8/pipe`, in_proj_qkv `TN128/S4/G80/pipe`, in_proj_z `TN128/S8/G48/pipe`, in_proj_b and in_proj_a the b+a stack's plan over the shared tile, gate and up alone `TN256/S4/G68/pipe`, o, out and down alone their split plans. A key outside the table is served by the MMA kernel, never by a default plan (one GROUPS step off cost 15–20 % in the sweep). The table is valid for the 20-core M5 Pro; another core count refuses with an INFO line until measured (`MEASURED_CORES`). The four-linear GDN call is served as two launches, qkv+z and b+a, each at its plan. The dual (gate+up) launch stays. Split counts are frozen per shape and never derived from the core count.
6. **Block default stays 8.** The tile's cost is flat to 16 rows (M = 16 costs 1.44–1.63× an 8-row call, 0.72–0.82× per row), so a deeper block may pay now where it did not on the MMA kernel; that is a sweep after this ships, not part of it, and decision 1's run-by-run service is what keeps such a sweep inside the contract.
7. **The fork key gains `ptile` (`active` while the MLP linears are slab-only, else `off`), read off the `_sous_slab` markers, and `projtile.py` joins `_EPOCH_FILES`; the tile's kernel files are excluded like kv8's.** The key field exists because decision 3 moves the 9–127-row MLP chunks from stock bits to int8 bits and the drafter that gates the conversion is not in the key (the key's docstring, which leaves the drafter out because the kernel routes a prefill the same with or without one, is amended); the epoch entry because the repack in `projtile.py` decides the path every MLP read takes. Nothing the tile itself computes is persisted: every fork and turn slot holds `prefill()` KV, the hybrid path restores the cache to the anchor after every decode, and `projection_tile.metal`/`.h` serve only inside the window. `vlm.py`, `projkernel.py`, `int8prefill.py` and the three re-addressed kernels change, so deploying this cold-starts the store once, as every kernel PR has.
8. **`sous tune` keys suite rows by the flag** (`Arm.projection_tile`, `SuiteRun.projection_tile`, `ArmSummary.key`), as for the tile, the kernel and kv8, since the flag changes numerics. `fit()` does not count the elective copy against the window; it reports the scope the guard would pick. The int8 winner-stage arm sets `projection_tile = false`, so the int8 grade measures int8 alone. The runner's `unavailable` notice is suppressed for the `no drafter` refusal, which every drafterless arm prints.

## Design

### Module layout

- `src/sous/engine/projtile.py` — the slab repack and its inverse, the slab-only conversion and revert, the stack builder, the plan table and `MEASURED_CORES`, the tile dispatch (single, stacked, dual and the two-launch GDN form), the decode window and the per-round flag, `availability()`, `enable()`, `clear()`, the probe, `calls`, the memory guard, `static_reason`, `fork_key_state()`. It holds no arrays at module level: no id-keyed maps, no `functools.cache` over arrays; every plane lives in a module's `__dict__`. Imports `projkernel` (tags, dispatch keys, `_refuse`) and `nax`; `projkernel` imports `projtile` function-locally inside its two seams and `int8prefill` reads the slab descriptor, so there is no cycle.
- `src/sous/engine/kernels/projection_tile.metal` and `projection_tile.h` — arm A's body: `matmul2d_descriptor(8, TN, 64, false, true, false, mode::multiply)` per quant group under `execution_simdgroups<S>`, the `simd_sum` row sums per 256-input block into the 256-byte double-buffered `input_sums` behind its three barriers (the prologue fill, the refill after a block's last group, the closing barrier per tile — persistent plans reuse the buffer across tiles, and the spike's mutation smoke could not see a missing prologue or closing barrier, so their presence rests on source review and the source hash below), the fp32 group epilogue `acc = fma(P, scale, acc); acc = fma(rowsum, bias, acc)`, groups in ascending order, `q4_traversal`'s validity guard on every element loop (dropping it wrote rows 8..15 out of bounds on the M5), the `DUAL` and `SPLITOUT` variants, and after a `// REDUCE` marker the fixed-order split reduce `sous_proj_tile_reduce` (`total = 0.0f; for s in 0..SPLITS-1: total += part[s]`, one bf16 rounding, never `mx.sum`); `remove_addrspace_t` on every cooperative-tensor `decltype`; `math_mode: safe` (measured at fast-math speed). Needs `nax.h` (Metal 4 MPP, macOS 26.2+). Derived from incoai/splash `q4_mpp_tiles.h` at 0d5a035 (Apache-2.0).
- `src/sous/engine/kernels/projection_mma.metal` gains a `SLAB` template switch in the plain and staged bodies (the slab spike's address formulas: unit `((n >> 8) * G + g) * 256 + (n & 255)`, 32 bytes of codes, one bf16 scale and bias) and `projection_row.metal` a `// SLAB` body (one lane per output column, 256 lanes per 256-column tile, each lane walking its column's groups in K order with the one-row kernel's exact arithmetic: the MMA order within a group, the fixed-tree group sum, the two-fma epilogue, the quarters `((a0 + a1) + a2) + a3`, one bf16 rounding). `q4_int8_gemm.metal` gains the slab address for its weight and scale reads (the same 32-byte unit per column and group; the scale plane read in place, which retires the per-call transposed copy for slab-only modules).
- Seams in existing files: `projkernel._weights()` returns a weight descriptor that carries the slab form; `linears()`, `_launch`, `_launch_row`, `_in_features`, `_dispatch_key`, `warm_up`, int8's `qmm` and `linear_int8` accept it; `projkernel._serve_verify` and `_serve_plain` ask `projtile.serves(...)` per run; `int8prefill._rows_ok` takes 9 rows and more on a slab-only module; `VLMEngine` enables the tile after `projkernel.enable_drafter` and before `kv8.enable`, opens its window inside `decode()` beside kv8's, closes it first in that `finally`, clears it in `unload()`, asserts no window is open at the top of `prefill()`, and passes the slab state to the fork key; `ManagedEngine` exposes the status; `LMEngine` reports `off`; `base.py` prints the load-line tokens and `_default_factory` takes the parameter; `config.py` parses the key; `tune/arms.py`, `tune/candidates.py`, `tune/decide.py` and the runner carry the suite key, `static_reason`, the scope report and the notice rule.

### The slab layout, the repack and the module forms

Per linear or stack, three planes from mlx's `weight` `[N, K/8]` uint32, `scales` and `biases` `[N, G]` bf16, N zero-padded to a multiple of 256 (`T = ceil(N/256)` tiles, `G = K/64` groups): codes `[T][G][256][8 words]` (each unit the 32 bytes of one column's one group, verbatim — low nibble first, no requantisation), scales `[T][G][256]` bf16, biases the same. The repack is `w.reshape(T, 256, G, 8).transpose(0, 2, 1, 3)` made contiguous and the planes `s.reshape(T, 256, G).transpose(0, 2, 1)`; its inverse is the same transpose back, and the round trip is bitwise. Padded columns hold zero codes, scales and biases and compute exactly 0. A stack concatenates its linears along N before padding and records each member's `(start, stop)`.

- **The weight descriptor.** `projkernel._weights(module)` returns `(w, scales, biases, slab)` where `slab` is `None` for an mlx-layout module and `SlabInfo(n_real, k, tiles)` for a slab-only one; every reader derives K and N from the descriptor, never from `w.shape`, and the dispatch and kernel caches key on the form. The test stand-ins (`tests/proj_fixtures.py`'s `stand_in_mma`/`stand_in_one_row`, `tests/test_int8prefill.py`'s `qmm` patches) inverse-repack a slab descriptor and run as before.
- **Slab-only modules (gate, up, down):** `module.weight`, `module.scales`, `module.biases` are replaced by the flat planes (1-D uint32 of `T·G·256·8` words; 1-D bf16 of `T·G·256`), so mlx's own `quantized_matmul` raises on rank rather than compute garbage from a 2-D array of the right shape; the module carries `_sous_slab = SlabInfo(...)` set with `object.__setattr__`. `eligible()` and int8's own eligibility run before the conversion (at their `enable()`), and the marker is what every reader tests afterwards. The conversion runs one linear at a time, `mx.eval` after each, mlx's arrays dropped before the next, `mx.clear_cache()` at the end: the peak is one linear's transient (gate/up 44.6 MB). The revert is the inverse repack per linear, bitwise, and deletes the marker; int8's tags and `_STAGE` and projkernel's tags need no restoring.
- **Copied modules (attention and GDN linears):** the stack's planes and slices hang off every member module as `_sous_tile = Stack(planes, slices, plan_key)` (one object shared by the members, set with `object.__setattr__`, out of mlx's parameter tree, freed with the modules at unload); mlx's arrays stay for prefill and the one-row kernel. A hook-2 call on one member inside the window runs the stack's launch and takes its slice (once per decode, the generation-prompt chunk). `copy_bytes(model)` sums the stacks' padded planes (4.13 GB on the 27B).

### The kernel and its plans

- **Template:** N (padded width written), K, TN (128 or 256), S (4 or 8), GROUPS (persistent threadgroups: `tile_index = group; < N/TN; += GROUPS`, a template int), PIPE, SPLITS (grid y; split s covers quant groups `[s·BLOCKS/SPLITS·4, (s+1)·BLOCKS/SPLITS·4)`), plus `DUAL` and `SPLITOUT`. Grid `(GROUPS·S·32, SPLITS, 1)`, threadgroup `(S·32, 1, 1)`; output `[8, N]` bf16, or `[SPLITS, 8, N]` fp32 partials under `SPLITOUT` followed by the reduce kernel (grid `(ceil(8N/256)·256, 1, 1)`, threadgroup 256). The only threadgroup allocation is `input_sums[64]` (256 bytes). Per element: row sum `s_g(r) = simd_sum(float(x[r][64g + l]) + float(x[r][64g + l + 32]))`, `P_g` = one `matmul2d` of the group's 64 exact products into a fresh fp32 cooperative tensor, `v = fma(float(P), float(scale), v); v = fma(s_g, float(bias), v)` in ascending group order from +0.0f, one bf16 rounding (the pipelined form keeps the order; G = 80, 96 and 272 are even, so its tail never runs). Rows ≥ T are zero rows of the padded `[8, K]` input (`pad8`, which allocates an `[8, K]` array per call under 8 rows and nothing at T = 8), so T is never a kernel argument.
- **Plans:** decision 5's table as a Python dict keyed by `(K, Ns)`; `_check` requires `K % 256 == 0`, `N % TN == 0` after padding, `TN ∈ {128, 256}`, `S ∈ {4, 8}`, `1 ≤ SPLITS ≤ K/256`; the dual requires `SPLITS = 1`. The table is pinned by a test that every dispatch key of the 27B resolves and that the per-key plans equal the spike's `plans_tuned.json` entries they were copied from.
- **Forms:** `run(x8, planes, n_pad, plan)` (one launch over one plane set, the stack sliced back by the caller), `run_dual(x8, gate_planes, up_planes, plan)`, and the GDN form `(qkv, z)` + `(b, a)`; `pad8` inside the measured call.

### Routing and the decode window

- `projtile.serves(rows, *, hook) -> bool` is `_active and _window is not None and _round_flag and (hook == 1 or 2 <= rows <= 8)`. `open_window()` and `close_window()` are called by `VLMEngine.decode()` where kv8's are (inside the draft-context `seed` block before `stream_generate`, and in that loop's `finally`); the tile's close runs first, in its own `try/finally`, and never raises, so a failure in kv8's close cannot leave the tile's window open; `prefill()` asserts the window is closed. `_round_flag` is the gate shim's per-round lever (read by `serves()`, never `_window`), 1 in production and restored to 1 at the start of every decode. `ManagedEngine._gen_lock` serialises every forward, so one module-global window suffices.
- `projkernel._serve_verify`: after its own conditions and its cut into runs of at most 8 rows, each run is served on the tile when `projtile.serves(rows, hook=1)` and every linear of the call carries planes (slab-only or a stack); a call with a linear without planes (the head; an attention or GDN call at scope `mlp`) keeps the MMA kernel whole — the verifier's calls group linears of one kind, so a call is served whole by one path; `calls["verify_tile"] += 1` per served run. Else the MMA path as today, reading the slab bodies for slab-only modules.
- `projkernel._serve_plain`: rows 2–8 with `projtile.serves(rows, hook=2)` and planes on the module → `projtile.plain(module, x)` (a stack member takes its slice), `calls["plain_tile"] += 1`; one row → the one-row kernel (slab body for slab-only modules); rows 9–127 on a slab-only module → `None`, so the call falls through to int8's wrapper, whose row floor admits it for a marked module (`calls["int8_small"] += 1` in int8's counters); otherwise as today. The drafter's own linears (`_DRAFT_TAG`) never reach the tile.
- `int8prefill.linear_int8`: a slab-only module reads its planes through the GEMM's slab address; `_rows_ok` is `rows >= MIN_ROWS or (rows >= 9 and slab)`; everything else unchanged.
- Everything else falls through as today. The only call that reaches mlx's `quantized_matmul` on a slab-only module is a bug, and it raises.

### Guards

`enable(model, *, config_value, kernel_status, tile_status, int8_status, drafter_kind, cache_budget, headroom_gb, reserve_gb)` runs on the `sous-model-load` thread, never raises, and its first statement clears `_active`, `_window`, `_round_flag`, the scope and the byte count. In order: the config (`false` → `off`); the projection kernel (`active`, else its reason — INFO); the attention tile (`active`, else its reason — INFO; this carries `nax.platform_reason()`); the drafter (`dflash`, else `no drafter` — INFO); int8 prefill (`active`, else `int8 prefill <state>` — INFO); the core count (`gpucores.read()` in `MEASURED_CORES`, else INFO); the model (every linear the tile would serve affine 4-bit gs64 with bf16 scales and biases, K % 256 == 0, every 27B dispatch key in the plan table — INFO); the source pins (`projkernel.VALIDATED_PROJ_SOURCES`, `verifyattn.VALIDATED_MLX`, the int8 GEMM's and the verifier's sources this module relies on — warning); `availability()` — the platform rule, then, on synthetic slab planes at every (K, N) the MLPs reach and every plan shape in the table, one compile and run of the tile and the reduce kernel, the slab MMA bodies, the slab one-row body and the int8 slab GEMM, success remembered (warning; this runs before anything is converted, so a toolchain that rejects a kernel never takes the revert path); the memory guard (below; the MLP conversion needs none); the conversion, then the copy; the warm-up (every (K, Ns, plan) the model reaches on the tile, the slab MMA bodies at T = 1..8 and the slab one-row kernel per MLP shape, the int8 GEMM at 9 and 128 rows on one slab-only linear); the probe. A probe failure or any exception (logged with its traceback, the exception's frames cleared before the revert so the probe's arrays are released first) is one `warnings.warn` plus `unavailable`, the conversion reverted and the copy dropped; if the revert itself raises, the markers stay authoritative (every reader is marker-driven) and the fork key reads them. INFO refusals say the tile does not apply to this load; warnings say it should have run.

**The memory guard.** With `config_value` `"mlp"` the copy is never made; with `"all"` it is made and the budget left is printed. Under `true`: with the automatic budget, the copy is made iff `headroom_gb − copy_gb − reserve_gb − 2 ≥ TILE_COPY_MIN_BUDGET_GIB` (14), where `headroom_gb` is `live_headroom()` read before the copy, which already contains the drafter; with an explicit `[model].prompt_cache_gb`, iff `headroom_gb − copy_gb − reserve_gb − 2 ≥ cache_budget` — the configured budget must still fit beside the copy — and never a warning, since the user chose the budget. A skipped copy is one INFO line naming the shortfall and scope `mlp`.

**The probe** (real linears: layer 3's q+k+v, o, gate+up, down; layer 0's qkv+z, b+a, out_proj): on the tile, every row at T = 1..8 `array_equal` to the same row at T = 1 through the same plan (T-invariance; the dual and the GDN two-launch form included); accuracy against a float32 reference built from the weights dequantised with scales and biases upcast to fp32: the floor is that reference rounded to bf16, and the tile's rel RMS must be at most `TILE_FLOOR_FACTOR` = 1.05× the floor on every probed linear (the spike's excess was 0.0009× the floor, so this catches a kernel that lost precision — a toolchain moving the group dot to reduced-precision intermediates — while block invariance alone compares the tile with itself; `REL_RMS_BOUND` = 1e-2 applies only to the synthetic cancelling layouts, where `quarter` at K = 17408 already reads 9.4e-3); a NaN planted in one row leaving the others bitwise unchanged; on the slab-only MLP linears, the slab MMA bodies at T = 1..8, the slab one-row kernel and the int8 GEMM at 9, 64 and 128 rows each `array_equal` to the mlx-layout kernel over the inverse-repacked planes (the conversion changed no bit); one decode-shaped call through both hooks with the window forced open (`calls` must show `verify_tile` and `plain_tile`), and the same call with the window closed served by the MMA path. It restores `calls`, leaves `_active` 0 and `mx.clear_cache()`s in `finally`. `projkernel.probe()`, which runs with no window open, keeps its parity step (MMA verify rows equal the one-row forward) unchanged.

### Memory

`copy_bytes(model)` is Σ over the stacks of `T·256 · K · 9/16` (4.13 GB on the 27B); the MLP conversion is size-neutral (17408 and 5120 are multiples of 256). `measure_cache_budget` runs after the copy as today and needs no change; the pressure valve reads live memory and sees the copy either way. The status document's `engine` block gains `projection_tile` (`state`, `scope`, `reason`, `copy_gb`, `probe_seconds`); `memory_gb` stays total active memory in GB of 1e9 bytes, documented as such.

### The contract, restated

With the tile active, the drafter's path is block- and batch-invariant: a position's logits do not depend on the block size or on which rows share a call (the tile's rows are independent, the attention tile and kv8 give a verify row its one-row bits, the GDN verifier is batch-invariant), so greedy output with the drafter is one function of the prompt for a given load and scope. Drafter-off greedy is another function of the prompt, differing by bf16 rounding inside the projections, so the two agree except at near-ties whose size the gate measures. With the tile refused or off — no DFlash drafter, int8 off, another Mac, `projection_tile = false` — drafter on equals drafter off bit for bit, as today. What changes in words: the attention tile's, kv8's and the projection kernel's bullets of `CLAUDE.md` that say "greedy parity with the drafter on or off holds because…" say "with the tile off, greedy parity with the drafter on or off holds because…; with it active, block invariance on the drafter's path holds because…"; `config.py`'s comments on the three flags, `docs/configuration.md`'s three paragraphs and the README's "Speed never changes the answer" say the same, with the gate's block-invariance figure. The attention tile's and kv8's own exactness (verify row == one-row call within their kernels) is unchanged, still probed, and still the reason their step boundaries and `verify_plan` cuts exist; the projection kernel's T-invariance, grouping invariance and one-row-equals-MMA remain bitwise, still probed, and are what keeps every path outside the window at today's bits.
